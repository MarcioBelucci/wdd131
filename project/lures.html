Lures & Techniques

Note: wording below is placeholder/draft content for you to personalize with your own explanations and experience — see the note at the end of this file.

Topwater Lures

Fished on the surface, these lures imitate struggling prey and trigger explosive strikes. They work best at dawn, dusk, or on overcast days when fish are feeding near the surface. Great for Tucunaré and Traíra around structure.

Jerkbaits & Minnow Lures

Mid-water lures retrieved with sharp jerks and pauses, imitating an injured baitfish. A strong current retrieve style works well for Dourado and Matrinxã in river sections.

Spinners & Spoons

Metal lures with flash and vibration that cover water quickly, useful for searching for active fish across a stretch of river or pond edge.

Soft Plastics & Jigs

Fished near the bottom or around structure, these lures are effective for larger, slower-moving species such as Pirarara and Tambacu that hold near cover.

Responsible Fishing

Always check current regulations for closed seasons (piracema), minimum catch sizes, and catch-and-release guidelines before heading out. Several species featured on this site, including Dourado, are subject to seasonal restrictions in Brazil.

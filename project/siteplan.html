Piracicaba Lures Anglers

Website Planning Document — WDD 131

1. Site Name

Piracicaba Lures Anglers

This name identifies a fictional sport fishing club based in Piracicaba, Brazil, dedicated to artificial lure fishing in local rivers and stocked fishing ponds. The name was chosen because it clearly states the club's location, its focus on lure fishing (as opposed to live bait), and its community aspect through the word anglers.

Logo concept: a simple circular wordmark with a stylized fishing lure/hook icon, shown above in the page header, reflecting a clean and modern outdoors-club identity.

Optional domain availability (not required for this course): piracicabalures.com

2. Site Purpose

The site provides a hub for sport fishing enthusiasts in the Piracicaba region by offering a guide to local fish species and the artificial lures best suited for each one, along with information about club events and a membership sign-up form for anglers who want to join the community.

3. Scenarios

  • As a beginner angler, I want to find which artificial lure to use for a specific fish species so that I can increase my chances of a successful catch.
  • As a visitor interested in the local fishing community, I want to find upcoming club events and a way to sign up so that I can join and meet other anglers.

4. Color Schema

The palette below reflects water, nature, and reliability, and is applied throughout this document.

  • Deep River Blue #0B3D5C — header background, footer, primary buttons
  • Reed Green #4F7B52 — section headings, icon accents
  • Sand #F4EFE6 — page and section backgrounds
  • Charcoal #232323 — body text
  • Lure Orange #E8793C — calls to action, links on hover

5. Typography

Two typefaces are used across the site, applied to this document as well:

  • Oswald — used for all headings (h1–h3). Bold and condensed, it reflects an outdoors-club identity and remains legible at large sizes.
  • Lato — used for body text, navigation, and form labels. A neutral, highly readable sans-serif for small screens.
